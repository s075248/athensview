Privacy Policy

This website has no user accounts and no tracking cookies. Here is exactly what happens with the information you enter.

Booking and enquiry forms

When you submit a booking request or message, the details you typed are placed in a pre-written WhatsApp or email message that you choose to send to Athens View reception. We only receive them when you press send in your own app. If the site owner has connected an optional form service, the same details are also delivered to the owner’s inbox.

Data stored in your browser

To remember your chosen dates between pages the site uses your browser’s session storage. Your last few booking requests are also kept in your browser’s local storage so you can find your reference number. This data never leaves your device through this site and can be removed by clearing site data.

Third-party services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, an embedded OpenStreetMap map on the Location page, and photos hosted online. Those providers may receive your IP address when the page loads. WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps links open those services under their own policies.
